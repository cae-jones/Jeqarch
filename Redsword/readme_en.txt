Redsword is a very lazy title.
It's also a 2D platformer with audio and braille output.
It's something of a reference to similar NES games, which is one reason it's so short. (though I doubt you'll be complaining about it being too short rather quickly, and instead be complaining about something else... no, I won't tell you what the something is.)


====================
=== How to Play ===
====================

(Please note: the keys mentioned in this readme are the defaults. They can be changed with the "configure keyboard" or "configure joystick" options from the main menu.)

---Navigation and movement:
This game is a 2d platformer, which means you will have to jump to and from platforms.
How might you do this?
Well, if you have a braille display connected to your computer, using US Computer Braille, you might be able to figure it out that way. You might want to set up your controls to a one-handed configuration, though, unless you're OK with the carpal acrobatics.
If you would rather go it audio-only, there are a few options available.
(Please remember that controls mentioned here are the defaults. You can change some of them.)

You always hear a distinct sound coming from the nearest ledge in the direction you're moving, if you are standing on something solid.
For walls, deadly drops, and platforms, you might try the sonar (o). It plays corresponding sounds at the nearest block it thinks is a platform/wall, so this should give you an idea of what's ahead.
If you have time to stop and look around in more detail, you can examine the area tile-by-tile by holding the cursor button (g) and moving the cursor with the directional controls.
You can hear a whole column at once by holding the cursor button and pressing an information button (h or r).
And if you really just need to pause the game and look around, you can hold the cursor and press interact (enter), and the game will remain paused until you press enter or escape.
Finally, you can press f6 to use a simple sonifier, which sweeps the level and plays a tone at each solid block, and nothing at open space. The sonifier will follow you horizontally, but it does not track you vertically, since it fits the entire screen rather easily.

If a platform is directly above you, trying to jump will just cause you to bump your head.
To jump onto a platform, you need to be out from under it, then jump toward it such that you are above it before you'd otherwise hit it.
If you release the jump button before you start to descend, your upward velocity will be cut in half. You can't quite stop by tapping jump (0 is only half of 0, after all), only rise more slowly.

(Of course, you should pay attention to friction and inertia. After all, it would be unfortunate if you finally landed on that platform, only to slide off because it's too slick!)

Some platforms move. It might help to familiarize yourself with how long you must hold left or right to move a single step, so as to not miss them. Fortunately, these are only crutial to two levels.


---Environmental Interaction:
You can open doors, activate switches, talk to people, and hold on to rope-ladders by pressing the interact button (enter).
There are some blocks which you must destroy with your sword. There is no way to tell how many attacks any block will take (unless you counted the last time you played).
There are some blocks which you can push. You only need to walk into them. (These blocks are completely ordinary to the cursor!)
Anyone standing under a falling block will be crushed!
You can certainly swim under water, but don't forget to come up for air!
And, of course, you want to avoid things like pits, spikes, and fire.


---Combat:
Your weapon is a red sword, which fills you with rage whenever it hits an enemy.
There are three ways to attack: standing, jumping, and crouching.
Crouching attacks are stronger, but slightly slower.
Jumping attacks are slightly faster.
If your opponent attacks with a weapon like a sword, you can block by swinging your sword at the same height.
For example, crouching (down) and attacking (space) can block a crouching attack, while you would just press attack to block a standing attack.
Of course, attacks are generally less than a third of a second in duration, so remember that you can dodge by jumping or crouching.
(Naturally, you can't crouch to dodge crouching attacks!)

Enemies will often attack you with projectiles.
You can crouch to avoid some projectiles.
Some projectiles will randomly hit you even if you're crouching, and others cannot be avoided by crouching at all.
If there's room to jump, though, jumping is usually the simplest way to avoid projectiles.

Some enemies can throw you.
If you find yourself thrown, you must crouch before you can use your sword again!
Remember, you must crouch, not only press down once or twice.
How long it will take for you to recover could be anywhere from 1 to 5 seconds!


---Rage:
The more you fight, the more enraged you become.
If your rage meter fills to 100%, you will rapidly attack in both directions, doing more damage than you normally would.
However, this rage burst is uncontrollable and unstoppable, so if you can plan for when it will happen, that might save you some frustration!
While fully enraged, your health recovers a little with every attack.
When you finally become calm again, you might find yourself disoriented and stumbling in the wrong direction. If you aren't careful, you might rage into a pit!
(Note: this is more dangerous when playing with a joystick, because it is harder to regain control of your motion.)


========================
=== Default Controls ===
========================

Keyboard:
Jump: up
Move left: left
Move right: right
Crouch or climb down: down
attack: space bar
interact (open doors, talk to people, use switches, etc): enter
Say health: h
say rage: r
Toggle sonar: o
cursor: g
quit: escape

Soni Playstation controller (you can use whatever joystick you like, but the buttons won't be guaranteed to be in the same order):
Attack: x
sonar: triangle
interact: square
Cursor: l1
rage: l2
health: r2
quit: start

System or immutable keys (these are not included in the joystick controls):
control: stop speech
Page up / Page down: adjust background music volume
Alt f4: exit, saves the current game.
f1: object viewer
f6: toggle sonifier
