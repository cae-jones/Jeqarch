
Mostly the same as SCS. These are SJ-specific:

This is how you can view story text line by line, like in the Oda Rising campaign:

story 0 text=this is the first line of the story
story 1 text=this is the second line of the stor
story 5 text=this is the sixth line of the story; it's 0-based.
read story

Adding items works just like in SCS, except the amount variable only affects arrows.

This adds 1 katana:

add item 1 10,10,25,25 katana


This adds a collection of 15 arrows:

add item 15 2,2,3,3 arrows

This adds 10 arcbuses, spread around the given area:

add items 10 20,20,3,30 arcbus


The map command loads saved maps in the maps folder, and places the player in district A0
To change the district, type "district", followed by an equals, followed by the value (a letter, then a number, no spaces). 10by10 is the maximum size.

district=a0

district=E9


You can change the number of districts in a map by typing the word "resize", followed by the word "map", followed by a space, the number of districts east to west, a comma, and the number of districts north to south. Like so:

resize map 2,3


You can add people in a few different ways:

add random samurai=2

^ adds two samurai at random locations in the current district.

add spot 10,20,20,30 shinobi=10

^ adds 10 shinobi to the area with a corner at (10, 20) and the other at (20, 30).

The possible classes you can use are:

shogun
daimyou
samurai
ronan
shinobi
kanoichi   (Makes the created Shinobi female)
civilian
man   (makes the created civilian male)
woman  (makes the created civilian female)
monk  (civilian with ihatsu)

If you use an unrecognized value, a civilian will be created.
(You can get male/female civilians with other words, like boy/girl.)


You cannot add people to zones, since there is no such thing, but you can add them to specific tile types, for example:

add tile shinobi=3 campsite
add tile civilian=10 house
add tile samurai=5 horse

The tiles you can use are:

clear
campsite
fire
water
deep_water
castle_wall
castle_room
courtyard
broken_wall
tree
hole
boulder
boat
horse
house
garden


Removing people is a little tricky, since you get to specify two things.
You can specify all people of a given class, all people in general... but with an optional flag, you can specify only enemies, only allies, only independents, or only people with a specific name.

remove people 10,10,25,25 samurai
remove people 0,0,50,50 shinobi neutral
remove people 5,10,15,20 civilian allies
remove people 30,10,40,25 any enemies
